
## May 2026

QuickView has been updated to version 2.7.4 with refined viewport control, a more compact slice selection panel, and various other small improvments in the UI.

## April 2026

QuickView version 2.6.1 has been released, providing long-desired features like [image download](/guides/quickview/miscellaneous#save-vis), [symmetric logarithmic scale](/guides/quickview/individual_views#linear-and-log-scales) for colorbars, generalized support for [arbitrarily dimensioned arrays](/guides/simulation_data#nd-vars), and substantially improved performance for [SCREAM output](/gallery/index#scream). 

## March 2026

QuickView and QuickCompare have been deployed to [NERSC](https://www.nersc.gov/) through [JupyterHub](https://jupyter.nersc.gov/) for further development and testing.
